Where this goes.

The 60-day pilot proves the pattern on one front door. Here's the arc that opens — shown honestly. None of this ships in the pilot; it's the direction the same idea points.

From one door to a connected pathway

The person doesn't live in one program. They cross all of them.

A single person with a substance use disorder rarely touches just "treatment." Over a year they may pass through a dozen funding and referral streams — each a silo, each with its own intake, each a place to lose the thread:

Coherence is the connective tissue between them — so a person's pathway stays whole as they move, instead of restarting at every seam. It maps almost exactly onto CalAIM's direction: no-wrong-door, closed-loop referrals, and cross-sector data exchange.

The flagship example — justice-involved reentry. Someone leaving custody has a narrow window to land in treatment, benefits, and housing before they fall back. Today those hand-offs are manual and brittle. A coherence layer keeps the connection intact across probation, treatment, and benefits — exactly the population CalAIM's reentry initiative is built to catch.

What makes it possible

You can't connect these without solving consent.

The reason this web has never been stitched together isn't ambition — it's confidentiality. SUD records carry special protection (42 CFR Part 2), and today most data-sharing runs on "trust, not verification." Connecting the pathway requires consent coherence: the right information moving only with the right permission, tracked and revocable.

That's the one hard thing we're already fluent in — it's the same Part 2 work behind the pilot's privacy design. The day-to-day wins (reach, capacity, cleaner data) and this larger vision are linked by it. It's an extension of what we do, not a leap.

A system that keeps improving

The approach gets better the more it's used.

Coherence isn't a fixed feature set — it's a way of measuring whether the whole system is better off, applied wherever a scarce human is buried in grind. Each place it's applied teaches the next. Across the kinds of systems public health runs, that compounds into real depth over time.

  • Reach, at scale — the same instrument pointed at more of the 95%, in more languages, through more front doors.
  • Coherence, at scale — the connected pathway above, one sector at a time.
  • Capacity, at scale — the grind lifted off more roles, so frozen headcount stops being the ceiling.
  • Equity, at scale — disparity measured and closed deliberately, not left to chance.

The logic underneath is simple: every part of the system keeps its own interests and constraints — and coherence finds the intersection where everyone wins. The resources already exist; coherence reclaims the waste.

Honest marker: everything on this page is direction. The pilot is a no-PHI patient front door, 60 days, nothing touching county systems. We never let the vision outrun what's actually true — that's the whole point of building on coherence.
